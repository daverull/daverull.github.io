Privacy Policy

KidCity (IE "KIDCITY", toy store, 270 Radostovtsa St., Almaty, Kazakhstan) operates the apps "KidCity Chats" and "KidCity Analytics". These are internal tools used only by KidCity staff. Last updated: 6 October 2026.

What data we process

  • Customer messages sent to KidCity on Instagram Direct and WhatsApp: the sender's username or phone number, message text and time.
  • Statistics of KidCity's own accounts on Instagram, Threads, YouTube and TikTok (views, followers, likes, audience geography).
  • Public content on Instagram and Threads (public posts found by hashtag or keyword, public profiles of businesses) — used for market research.

How we use it

  • To read customer questions and reply to them. Replies are sent only by a KidCity staff member in response to a customer's message. We do not send unsolicited or bulk messages.
  • To measure the quality of our customer service (response time, unanswered questions) and to improve our product range.
  • To understand which content topics and formats perform well, so we can plan our own posts. We do not publish, like or comment on other people's content through these tools.

We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising profiles.

Service providers

Data is processed on our behalf only by: Cloudflare, Inc. (hosting and database, USA); Anthropic, PBC (AI-assisted analysis of messages for KidCity's internal needs, USA); Google LLC (storage of internal reports, USA). They may use the data only to provide their services to KidCity.

Retention

Messages and statistics are kept for up to 24 months for internal analysis, then deleted.

Data deletion

To request deletion of your data (for example, your messages to KidCity), email kidcity.kz@gmail.com with your Instagram username or WhatsApp number. We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also remove the app's access at any time: Facebook/Instagram — Settings → Apps and Websites; Google — myaccount.google.com/permissions; TikTok — Settings → Security → Apps.
